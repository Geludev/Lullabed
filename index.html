Lullabed – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · App: Lullabed – Competitive Sleeping (Android) · Contact: gelubdev@gmail.com

Lullabed is built so that your sleep data stays on your phone. There is no Lullabed account and no Lullabed server. This policy explains what the app processes, where it goes and what you control.

1. Data that stays on your phone

  • Microphone audio is analysed live on the device to detect movement, snoring, sleep talk and other sounds. Audio is never uploaded. Only short clips of detected sounds are saved on your phone (if you enabled recording); they are deleted automatically after the period you choose, except clips you mark as favourites.
  • Motion and light sensors are read during tracking to estimate sleep stages and bedroom light.
  • Sleep data (nights, stages, scores, notes, mood, alarm settings, your name and age if you enter them) is stored only in the app's private storage.
  • Crash logs are saved on the phone only. Nothing is sent automatically; you can choose to share a report yourself.

2. Data that leaves your phone – only when you use these features

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Friends, rankings, challenges, cheers (optional) Your chosen name, profile picture choice, sleep score and – only if you allow it per friend – sleep duration and times, sleep graph, sleep notes, bedtime status and one sound clip per night. Only the friends you add. The data is end-to-end encrypted on your phone and delivered through public Nostr relays (e.g. relay.damus.io, nos.lol, relay.primal.net). Relays can see technical metadata such as your random public key, timestamps and IP address, but not the content.
Sleep together / partner mode (optional) Sound levels and timings (never audio) and your sleep score. Directly to your partner's phone over your local Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet.
Weather (optional) Approximate location (rounded coordinates). Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to fetch the local weather.
Place of a night / home location (optional) Approximate location. Android's built-in geocoder (provided by your phone's system services) to find the town name.
Stories A normal download request (and, at most once a month, a check whether a recording has moved). Internet Archive (archive.org), which hosts the LibriVox recordings.
Friend server list (only if you use Friends) A normal download request, at most once a week. No personal data. GitHub Pages (geludev.github.io), which hosts the current list of Nostr relays so the app keeps working when a relay shuts down.
Health Connect (optional) Sleep sessions and stages are written to Health Connect; steps and heart rate are read from it. Health Connect on your phone. Data from Health Connect is only used to show your sleep statistics and is never sent anywhere.
Backup (optional) A backup file of your sleep data. Only the folder you pick (for example a cloud drive you use).

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No account and no collection of your data by the developer.

4. Permissions

Microphone (sleep tracking), notifications (tracking status and alarm), exact alarms and full-screen alarm (to wake you on time), running in the background all night, camera (only to scan a friend's QR code), approximate location (only for weather and place), Health Connect (optional sync). Every optional permission can be refused or revoked in Android's settings.

5. Your control and deletion

  • Settings → Your data → Delete all removes every night, statistic and recording from the phone.
  • Removing a friend replaces your shared card with an empty one. Uninstalling the app deletes all app data on the phone.
  • Data already delivered to friends' phones is stored on their devices.

6. Children

Lullabed is not directed at children under 13.

7. Medical note

Lullabed is not a medical device. Sleep stages and breathing indications are estimates and are not a diagnosis.
